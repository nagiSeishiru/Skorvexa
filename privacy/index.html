Privacy Policy

Skorvexa is built to work without knowing who you are. We don't ask for an account, we don't show ads, and we don't collect personal data. This policy explains what that means in practice.

01Who we are

Skorvexa ("Skorvexa", "we", "us") is a mobile app for iPhone and iPad that shows live football scores, fixtures, standings and match statistics. This policy applies to the Skorvexa app and to this website.

02Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. In particular, Skorvexa:

  • has no user accounts and never asks for your name, email address or phone number;
  • shows no advertising and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools;
  • does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone;
  • does not sell, rent or share information about you with anyone.

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to reply to you and delete the conversation once it's no longer needed.

03Information stored on your device

To work properly, the app keeps a small amount of information in its own storage on your device:

  • the teams, competitions and matches you follow;
  • your score predictions and points in the "Call it" game;
  • your notification settings and whether you've completed the welcome screens.

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

04Match data and images

Scores, fixtures, lineups and statistics, together with club crests, competition logos and player photos, are provided by a third-party sports data provider. To show them, the app requests them from the provider's servers.

Like any internet request, these requests include your device's IP address, which is needed to deliver the response. They do not contain your name, an account, an advertising identifier or any other information that identifies you, and we do not use them to build a profile of you.

05Notifications

Notifications are optional and off until you turn them on. Kickoff reminders and goal or full-time alerts are created on your device from the match data the app has downloaded. We don't operate a notification server and don't receive a notification token. You can change or turn off notifications at any time in the app's Settings or in iOS Settings.

06Services provided by Apple

Rating the app uses Apple's built-in rating window, and sharing the app uses the iOS share sheet. Downloads, ratings and reviews are handled by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive personal information from these features. Apple's handling of information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

07Links to other websites

The app and this website may link to other websites, such as the App Store. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those websites.

08Your rights

Depending on where you live, data protection laws such as the GDPR or Türkiye's KVKK may give you rights to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. Because Skorvexa does not collect personal data, there is normally nothing for us to provide or delete. If you have emailed us and want that conversation deleted, contact us and we'll do so.

09Children

Skorvexa is suitable for all ages. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

10Security

All connections between the app and the data provider are encrypted using HTTPS. Information stored by the app stays within your device's protected app storage.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when we add new features. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

12Contact

If you have any questions about this policy or your privacy, email support@koraplus.anonaddy.com.
