Privacy Policy

Short version: Skorvexa has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your choices stay on your iPhone.

01The details

Who we are

Skorvexa is an independent iPhone app for football scores, tables and statistics. It does not require sign-up or login.

Personal data we collect

None. There are no user accounts, we show no ads, and the app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. We do not sell or share data, because we do not have any.

Data stored on your device

These stay in the app's private storage on your iPhone and are never uploaded:

  • the teams, leagues and matches you follow;
  • your score predictions and points in the free “Call it” game;
  • notification preferences and whether you completed onboarding.

Deleting the app removes all of it.

Network requests

To show scores, the app downloads football data from an external data provider, and team, league and player images from that provider's media server. As with any website, these servers receive your device's IP address with each request. Requests contain no account, advertising or device identifiers.

Notifications

Kickoff reminders and goal or result alerts are created on your device from the match data the app has downloaded. We do not run a push server and do not receive a push token. You can turn alerts off in the app's Settings or in iOS Settings.

Ratings and sharing

“Rate Skorvexa” shows Apple's own rating sheet. “Share the app” uses the iOS share sheet. All of this is handled by Apple; we receive nothing from it. Ratings and reviews are covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Links to other sites

Support and legal links open in Safari. The policies of external sites are not covered by this policy.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy? See the Support page.
