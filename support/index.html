02Frequently asked

How often are live scores updated?

During live matches the app refreshes every 30 seconds while it's open. Pull down on any list to refresh straight away.

How do I follow my team?

Open the team's page and tap the star (for a league, tap Follow). Followed clubs, leagues and matches are shown first on FIELD and in MATCHES, and can send alerts if notifications are on.

Why didn't I get a goal alert?

Alerts are created on your iPhone from fresh match data, so the app needs to be open or recently used. Check that notifications are on in Settings → Notifications and in iOS Settings → Skorvexa. Kickoff reminders are scheduled ahead and arrive even when the app is closed.

A league, lineup or stat is missing.

Coverage depends on our data provider. Lineups and detailed statistics may be unavailable for lower leagues or friendlies. You can find any covered league with Search.

Some clubs show initials instead of a crest.

Crests load from the data provider. If one is unavailable, or you're offline, the app shows the club's initials instead.

Is “Call it” betting?

No. It's a free prediction game: 3 points for the exact score, 1 for the right result. There is no money, no odds and no prizes.

Can I use a light theme?

Skorvexa is designed as a dark app, like a stadium at night, so there's a single dark theme.

Do you store my data?

No. Your follows, predictions and settings stay on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.
