02Scores and matches

How often are live scores updated?

While a match is being played and the app is open, scores refresh every 30 seconds. You can pull down on any screen to refresh straight away.

Which competitions does Skorvexa cover?

33 competitions: the Süper Lig, 1. Lig, 2. Lig, Turkish Cup and Turkish Super Cup; the Champions League, Europa League, Conference League and UEFA Super Cup; the World Cup, European Championship, Nations League and their European qualifiers, and the Club World Cup; the top leagues of England, Spain, Italy, Germany, France, the Netherlands, Portugal, Belgium, Scotland, Saudi Arabia and the United States, plus the English Championship; and the main domestic cups of England, Spain, Italy, Germany and France.

A score or statistic looks wrong.

Match data comes from a professional sports data provider and is usually corrected within a few minutes. If it still looks wrong after the match, email us with the teams and the date.

Why are lineups or statistics missing for some matches?

Lineups are published about an hour before kickoff, and detailed statistics are not available for every competition or match. They appear in the app as soon as they're released.

Some clubs show initials instead of a crest.

When a crest can't be loaded, for example on a slow connection, the app shows the club's initials instead. It usually fixes itself the next time the screen loads.

03Following and alerts

How do I follow a team, league or match?

Open the team's page and tap the star, open a league and tap Follow, or open a match and tap the bookmark. Everything you follow is shown first on the home screen and in Matches.

How do I turn on match alerts?

Go to You, tap the gear icon to open Settings, then choose Notifications and allow them. You can choose kickoff reminders, goals and full-time results separately.

Why didn't I get a goal alert?

Goal and full-time alerts are delivered while Skorvexa is open or was used recently, so they can be delayed if the app has been closed for a long time. Kickoff reminders are scheduled in advance and arrive even when the app is closed. Also check that notifications are allowed in iOS Settings → Skorvexa → Notifications.

04Predictions

How does "Call it" work?

Before kickoff, predict the final score of a match. When the match ends you get 3 points for the exact score or 1 point for the right result (win, draw or loss). Points raise your fan level.

Is "Call it" gambling?

No. It's a free game played for fun. There are no stakes, no odds, no prizes and nothing to buy, and points have no monetary value.

05Ads, privacy and the app

Why does Skorvexa show ads?

Ads keep Skorvexa free. We keep them limited: never on the first launch, a minimum gap between full-screen ads, and only family-friendly content.

How do I stop personalised ads?

Choose "Ask App Not to Track" when iOS asks, or turn tracking off any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you can also change your consent in Settings → Privacy choices.

I saw an inappropriate ad.

Sorry about that. Use Settings → Report an ad, or email support@koraplus.anonaddy.com with what the ad showed and when you saw it, and we'll block it.

Do I need an account?

No. Skorvexa works without an account or sign-in.

Does Skorvexa collect my data?

We don't. The teams you follow, your predictions and your settings stay on your device. Our advertising partner, Google AdMob, may collect some data to show ads; the Privacy Policy explains what and how to opt out.

How do I reset the app?

Deleting and reinstalling Skorvexa removes everything stored on your device, including the teams you follow and your predictions.

Is Skorvexa an official app?

No. Skorvexa is independent and is not affiliated with the TFF, UEFA, FIFA or any league, club or federation.
