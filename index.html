Inside the app

Live scores

Goals, cards and the minute, refreshed every 30 seconds while a match is on.

Match numbers

Possession, shots, xG, both starting elevens with player ratings, and the timeline of every event.

Title races

Who's chasing who at the top, plus full standings, fixtures by round, top scorers and form.

Your club first

Follow teams and matches, and they come first on the home screen and in the fixture list.

Alerts

A reminder 15 minutes before kickoff, then goals and the final score for the matches you follow.

Call it

Predict the score before kickoff. 3 points for the exact score, 1 for the result. Free, and no betting.
